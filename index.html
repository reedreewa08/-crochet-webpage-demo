THE STORY OF FIL DE LUNE

Every stitch has its own
back story.

Fil de Lune is a small crochet studio inspired by slow creation, delicate details, and the beauty of things made by hand. I got the inspiration to start the project after having one of toughest lesson in life that carved me in a beatiful soul. Just like me my crochet journey might be hard but at the end it becomes a beautiful piece.
